The Operational Gap

LEGACY AIR DEFENSE FAILS AGAINST AUTONOMOUS UAVs

The battlefield has changed. Small, low-altitude drones and unmanned maritime vessels operate below the detection threshold of conventional systems. The window to respond is measured in seconds. And conventional radar gives away your position.

Active Radar Exposes You

It acts like an electronic lighthouse. Adversaries immediately pinpoint, geolocate, and target emitting sensors using anti-radiation weaponry.

RF Jamming Creates Collateral Damage

Active frequency jammers disrupt civilian emergency bands and air traffic control. Legally prohibited near airports and urban cores.

Low-Altitude Blind Spots

Low-cost, quiet autonomous drones fly beneath standard radar horizons, easily bypassing traditional perimeter defense grids.
